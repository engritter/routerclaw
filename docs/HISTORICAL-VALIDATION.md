## What ran on the isolated VPS

The recorded laboratory used a declared modified development host, a restricted dedicated service and local interface, plus the official unmodified Codex 2026.11.1 plugin marked trusted. The real completions used the owner's OAuth subscription route; API-key environment and fallback candidates were absent in that recorded configuration. This establishes the prepared route observed there, not actual quota consumption or invoice savings.

Two earlier ingress attempts were refused for missing leases. One filesystem-denial scenario only elicited a verbal refusal; it does **not** prove technical filesystem isolation. A return procedure was prepared, but a full rollback exercise was not established. Astra/medium was not separately exercised as a real isolated-VPS route. Later explicit-model comparisons temporarily disabled routing; those results remain a separate experiment.

## The 48 explicit-model executions

- **16 executions:** eight models at two efforts. All completed, but only 14 initially passed the read/write tool task. Luna/low and Sol/low initially failed, claiming the tools were unavailable. Later guided successes do not erase those failures.
- **24 executions:** eight models across three synthetic scenarios: customer service, uncertainty and review. All passed the corrected semantic assessment. Inputs varied; this was not a controlled identical-input performance benchmark.
- **Eight additional executions:** typed JSON-contract tasks; all passed. The original oracle had required a numeric field without that type being specified in the original prompt. The original results were retained, the semantic correction was separate, and the typed-contract tasks were separate runs.

The three datasets contain 48 distinct run IDs with no overlap. Counts show breadth of exercised paths, not a universal model ranking. Reported elapsed times are descriptive observations; no causal speed, quality, cost or quota comparison was established. Planned negative matrices of 19 routing and 12 filesystem cases were not silently counted as executed tests.

## Counting and provenance

Successive suite sizes are snapshots, not numbers to sum. The same 47 sanitized cases run on Linux and Windows remain 47 cases, not 94. The eight earlier and four new Telegram inputs belong to different policy revisions. Historical results retain their actual selections, including the previous Astra/medium role; changing today's rule does not rewrite that evidence.

The [metadata ledger](../evidence/historical-validation.json) names the inspected report categories. These are author-operated records reviewed with the team's agent, not an independent security audit or third-party certification. The packaged 47-case reference is reproducible offline; the private live observations are not reproduced merely by running it.
