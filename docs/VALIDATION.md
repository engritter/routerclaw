## Defects found during real validation

- The idle dispatcher initially bypassed the follow-up-only wrapper. It was corrected after native admission/adoption, not by fabricating a new user source.
- Planned model choice was presented as fallback against the configured default. Accounting was bound to the admitted turn/run; genuine fallback behavior was retained.
- Administrative inference inherited the parent's custom scope. Isolation was restricted to the authorized native administration task.
- A native policy preparation refusal and a separate classifier timeout occurred. The final revision added narrowly scoped pre-prompt recovery and increased the classifier deadline from 15 to 45 seconds.

## What remains unproven

1. Universal native slash-command interception. A real `/status` request answered outside per-message inference, showing configuration defaults. This contradicted the earlier universal-refusal wording; it remains a gap, not a passed test.
2. The precise root cause of the native preparation race. Locally qualified recovery was not exercised by the eight later live inputs.
3. Real production stress behavior of the new classifier deadline.
4. General audio, image, video, callback-menu and cancellation-command coverage. Local abort/authority fixtures do not demonstrate every native command path.
5. Absence of native post-terminal context snapshot warnings. `invalid_content` warnings persisted despite successful outcomes and delivery.
6. Cost, quota savings, throughput, broad compatibility, production reliability, or an independent security audit.

The native terminal outcomes and response deliveries establish the listed successes. They do not erase these boundaries.
